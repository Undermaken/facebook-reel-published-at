# Privacy policy / Informativa sulla privacy / Política de privacidad / Datenschutzerklärung / Politique de confidentialité

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This policy covers the Facebook Reel dates browser extension. Controller: Matteo Boschi. Contact: [mbmaken@hotmail.it](mailto:mbmaken@hotmail.it). A standalone [HTML version](privacy-policy.html) is also available.

## English

The extension's only purpose is to show the publication date and time of the Facebook Reel you are viewing, with optional elapsed time and a Facebook-provided AI indication.

On `www.facebook.com`, it checks the current page address to identify Reel pages. On a Reel page, it reads the page's already-loaded metadata, including the Reel/video ID, owner ID, shareable URL, publication timestamp, and explicit AI flag when present. It observes the page's own GraphQL responses and initial page data. Responses may also contain suggested Reels; candidate video IDs, timestamps, and AI flags can enter the short-lived cache to identify the active Reel. Other response fields are not retained. It does not make extra requests to Facebook, access cookies, or read other websites. Outside a Reel page, it watches the URL and local preferences but does not inspect GraphQL bodies or page metadata.

The extension uses this information only to match the active Reel and display its date. Reel metadata stays in bounded memory; each entry expires 30 minutes after its latest observation, and the cache clears when you leave the Reel page or close the tab. Your display preferences, such as position, colors, size, and optional labels, are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your device until you remove the extension or clear its storage. The current Reel address is not saved as browsing history.

The extension has no account, backend, analytics, advertising, or data sale. It does not send Reel metadata or preferences to the developer or other third parties. Facebook's own page requests continue under Facebook's policies. The extension uses data only for its visible date feature and follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy's Limited Use requirements. You can turn off the display in settings and remove the extension in Chrome at any time.
