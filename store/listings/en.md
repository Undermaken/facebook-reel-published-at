# English store listing

**Locale:** `en` (default)

**Name from manifest:** Facebook Reel dates

**Short description from manifest:** Show publication dates on Facebook Reels, with your preferred position and appearance.

## Detailed description

See the publication date and time beside the Facebook Reel you are watching. The extension reads metadata that Facebook already loads and formats the date in your browser's language and local time.

Choose where the date appears: below the creator, at the top center of the Reel, or below the Reel. Set the text size, text color, background color, and border color. You can also show elapsed time and an AI badge when Facebook explicitly flags that Reel.

The extension shows nothing while the date is loading. It reports an unavailable date only when the matching Reel's metadata explicitly says the date is absent. Some Reels do not provide usable publication metadata.

It works on `www.facebook.com/reel/<id>`. Appearance preferences stay in local browser storage. Reel metadata stays in temporary memory. The extension has no separate account, backend, analytics, or extra Facebook requests.

Independent extension. Not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.
